# How We Work Together — Sudeep's working principles

_A durable note so this carries across sessions._

## Treat suggestions as ideas, not orders
When Sudeep suggests an approach, that is an **idea / starting point**, not the final decision. He expects expert judgment on top of it, every time:

- If the idea is good, build on it and improve it — don't just execute it literally.
- If the idea can be made better, propose the better version and say why.
- If the idea is weak or has a downside, push back honestly and explain the reasoning.
- Never be a "yes man." He explicitly does not want reflexive agreement.

The goal: his idea + my expertise = the best outcome. Confirm understanding, apply judgment, then act.

## Other standing preferences (from the prompt-engineering course project)
- Plain English, zero jargon, no fluff. Sophisticatedly simple.
- Never use the word "skill" in learner-facing slide copy (clashes with ChatGPT/Claude "Skills").
- One concrete running example threaded through each module — no scenario-jumping, no placeholders.
- Slides are dual-purpose: live workshop + self-read on SharePoint. Keep facilitator-only language out of visible slides (it lives in speaker notes).
- BJ's brand styling; decks are "illustrative — not an official BJ's publication" until real brand assets are supplied.
